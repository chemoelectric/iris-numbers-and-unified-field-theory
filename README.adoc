A textbook series on a constructive number system that contains as
part of its structure a unified field theory for physics.

Included also is an ACL2 “book.” This is a library for the ACL2 proof
assistant that is written in Common Lisp, and which is much friendlier
than most proof assistants are. And there is a directory of small
programs related to what appears in the volumes.

* _The Iris Number System, Volume I: Fundamentals_
* _The Iris Number System, Volume II: Applications to Number Theory, Analysis, Probability Theory, Statistics_
* _The Iris Number System, Volume III: Applications to Geometry, Algebra, Representations, Topology, Lattices, Categories, Combinatorics_
* _The Iris Number System, Volume IV: Applications to Physics, Chemistry, Astronomy, Engineering, Communications, Time-Binding_
* _The Iris Number System, Volume V: Applications to Spectral Theory, Circuit Theory, Transmission Theory, Electronics, Computing_

The Iris number system replaces continuous real numbers with
indefinitely finely divided discrete ones, corresponding to interval
numbers in computing. Differential calculus is done in the manner of
nonstandard analysis, but constructively: the discrete examination of
detail is done again, but with finer and finer “instruments.” Thus the
mechanical intuition is that of ε-δ—there is no blather about
“infinitesimals”—but the operations performed are the intuitive ones
of nonstandard analysis, requiring no special rules for zero-over-zero
or infinity-over-infinity.

The Law of Excluded Middle is not excluded. You will find proofs by
contradiction in the texts.

The unified field theory replaces all current theories, including the
gravitational, strong, and weak forces, with just Maxwell’s equations
and the force of electromagnetism. Mass is shown to be a self-induced
electromagnetic effect, with inertial and gravitational mass being the
same thing. Gravitation is a coupling effect between overlapping
portions of the electromagnetic field.

All of the universe is filled with an electromagnetic field. There is
no vacuum. There is no “curvature,” either. Space and time are as one
intuitively imagines them. “Space-time” is an artifact that came from
Einstein implicitly assuming the existence of a luminiferous ether
that does not move. This was not a correct solution to Michelson’s and
Morley’s observation that there is no “ether wind.” A correct solution
is more like that of Walther Ritz—light has momentum—but my education
was in electrical engineering, not physics, so _I_ remembered to treat
electromagnetic as _waves in a field_, not as projectiles.

Fundamental constants, such as the electron charge-to-mass ratio, the
proton-to-electron mass ratio, Newton’s gravitational constant, etc.,
are derived from first principles. Thus the unified field theory that
results from the new insight is almost certainly correct.
